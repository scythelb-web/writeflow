## Detailed Description

WriteFlow is an AI-powered writing assistant that works everywhere you type. Highlight text in any text field — Gmail, Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, Google Docs, anywhere — and transform it instantly with AI.

**What WriteFlow can do:**

✨ **Rewrite Professionally** — Make your writing polished, clear, and confident
💬 **Make Casual** — Tone it down for friends, social media, or chat
✓ **Fix Grammar** — Instantly correct spelling, grammar, and punctuation
📝 **Make Shorter** — Cut the fluff and get to the point
📖 **Expand** — Add detail and substance to thin text
🌐 **Translate to English** — Instantly translate any language into English

**How it works:**

1. Paste your OpenAI API key in Settings (one-time setup)
2. Highlight text in any text field on any website
3. Right-click → pick your action, or use the popup
4. AI replaces the text inline — done in seconds

**Why WriteFlow?**

- Works EVERYWHERE — Gmail, Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, Notion, Google Docs, WordPress, and more
- No subscriptions, no accounts — you use your own OpenAI key, we never see your data
- Right-click or popup — use whichever is faster for you
- Dark theme, clean UI — stays out of your way
- 10 free uses per day, unlimited with Pro ($5/mo)

**Privacy:**

Your API key is stored locally on your computer. Text is sent directly from your browser to OpenAI. WriteFlow never sees, stores, or logs your content. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics.

**Pro Upgrade ($5/month):**

- Unlimited daily use
- Custom tones and saved prompts (coming soon)
- Rewrite history (coming soon)
