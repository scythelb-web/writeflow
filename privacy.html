WriteFlow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

What WriteFlow Is

WriteFlow is a Chrome extension that provides AI-powered writing assistance — rewriting, grammar fixing, translation, and more. It works by sending text you select to OpenAI's API for processing.

What Data We Collect

We collect nothing. WriteFlow does not collect, store, transmit, or log any of your data. There are:

  • No user accounts
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No cookies
  • No server-side logging of your text

What Is Stored Locally

The following data is stored exclusively on your device using Chrome's local storage and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your OpenAI API key — so you don't have to re-enter it
  • Your daily usage count — to track the 10/day free tier limit
  • Your Pro license key — if you upgrade to Pro

You can clear this data at any time by removing the extension from Chrome.

How Your Text Is Processed

When you use WriteFlow to rewrite, fix grammar, or translate text:

  1. Your selected text is sent directly from your browser to OpenAI's API (api.openai.com)
  2. OpenAI processes the text according to their API terms and returns the result
  3. WriteFlow inserts the result back into your text field

WriteFlow's servers never see, touch, or relay your text. The communication is entirely between your browser and OpenAI.

Use of OpenAI's API is subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

Pro Subscriptions

If you choose to upgrade to WriteFlow Pro ($5/month):

  • Payments are processed by Stripe — we never see or store your credit card details
  • We store your email address and a license key to verify your Pro status
  • You can cancel anytime via Stripe's customer portal

Stripe's use of your payment information is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

WriteFlow relies on these third-party services:

  • OpenAI — for AI text processing (only when you use a WriteFlow action)
  • Stripe — for payment processing (only if you upgrade to Pro)

Your Rights

Since we don't collect your data, there's nothing to request, export, or delete. If you've subscribed to Pro and want your account deleted, contact us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and bump the date above. Significant changes will be noted in the extension's Chrome Web Store listing.
